## Selling the product (any country)

- **You do not need a patent to sell software** in the US, EU, Japan, or elsewhere.
- Copyright protects your **expression** (code, copy, UI). Trademark protects **brand confusion** (name/logo). Patents are optional monopoly rights on **inventions** — separate from sales rights.
- A public repo since 2026-05-27 may affect **whether you can still file patents** in some countries (many require absolute novelty; the US has a limited grace period for your own disclosure). That is **not** the same as “US-only sales.”

## Practical moat (what actually protects the business)

| Moat | Notes |
|------|--------|
| **Local-first architecture** | Libraries and credentials stay on the user’s machine — no BAKLOG cloud to breach. |
| **Cross-store normalization** | Years of fetcher + dedupe + ownership-aware deals — hard to replicate quickly. |
| **Trust & positioning** | “One honest backlog across every store” — privacy story is product truth, not marketing fluff. |
| **Execution & community** | Speed of shipping, invite quality, and word-of-mouth — not a patent filing. |
