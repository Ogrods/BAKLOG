## What BAKLOG is

BAKLOG is a local-first desktop tool that imports game libraries and wishlists from storefronts you connect, and shows backlog, deals, and related views on your machine. The default server is a local process on your computer (`127.0.0.1`). Catalog JSON and store credentials stay on your machine unless you explicitly enable an optional hosted feature (for example Pro cloud sync).
