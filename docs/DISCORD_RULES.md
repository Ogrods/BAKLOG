## Main rules (permanent)

**Welcome to the BAKLOG community**

BAKLOG helps you track games across stores. Everything runs on your machine. This server is for help, feedback, and chat around the project.

By participating here, you agree to these rules and to [Discord's Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms) and [Community Guidelines](https://discord.com/guidelines).

---

**1. Be respectful**
Treat others the way you want to be treated. Disagreement is fine; personal attacks, slurs, and harassment are not.

**2. No hate or discrimination**
No racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia, ableism, or other targeted hate. No "jokes" at someone's expense.

**3. No spam or self-promotion**
No unsolicited ads, referral spam, repeated pings, or "join my server" posts without staff approval. One account per person.

**4. Stay on topic**
Use the channel that fits your message. Setup in #setup-help, store Connect in #connections-help, bugs in #bug-reports, ideas in #feature-requests, chat in #general.

**5. English primary**
This server runs in English so staff and most members can help. Short code or log snippets in other languages are fine.

**6. No NSFW or illegal content**
No porn, gore, or content that breaks the law in your region or Discord's rules.

**7. No piracy or cracked stores**
Do not share pirated games, cracked launchers, or instructions to bypass store authentication. Talking about games you own is welcome.

**8. Protect private data**
Never post your or anyone else's:
- Store logins, passwords, or 2FA codes
- Session cookies, NPSSO, API keys, or `.env` contents
- BAKLOG credential files or unredacted Connect browser dumps
- Personal info (real name, address, phone) without consent

If you need help with Connections, describe the store and error. Do not paste secrets. Staff may delete unsafe posts without warning.

**9. Report bugs the right way**
Use #bug-reports with OS, BAKLOG version, steps, and what you expected. Redact paths and tokens from screenshots and logs.

**10. Follow staff direction**
Mods may warn, timeout, kick, or ban to keep the server usable. Their calls on server membership are final. Appeals go to the owner in DM.

**11. No drama or brigading**
No callouts, witch hunts, or coordinating harassment against other people, communities, or companies.

**12. Have fun**
Share backlog screenshots, tips, and what you are playing. We are here to enjoy games and make BAKLOG better.

---

Questions? Ask in #setup-help or DM a mod. Thank you for helping keep this a safe, useful place.

**Beta testing (temporary)**

You are in early access. Builds may break, data formats may change, and features may disappear.

- **Expect rough edges** - Known issues are listed in #known-issues.
- **Feedback welcome** - Bugs in #bug-reports, ideas in #feature-requests. GitHub is the long-term record when we file issues from Discord.

When BAKLOG leaves beta, this section will be removed. The rules above stay the same.
