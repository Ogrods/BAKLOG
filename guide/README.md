## What BAKLOG covers

BAKLOG pulls **twelve libraries** and **eight wishlists** across **19 store connections** (27 fetcher jobs total). Supported stores include Steam, GOG, PlayStation, Epic, Amazon, Xbox, Battle.net, Ubisoft Connect, Nintendo Switch, itch.io, Humble Bundle, and EA App.

For a product overview, screenshots, and the invite waitlist, see [baklog.app](https://baklog.app) or the repo [README](../README.md).
