Questions

Honest answers.

Is it safe?

Your credentials stay encrypted on your machine. Store fetches use your own browser session and your IP. There is no BAKLOG server holding your logins.

Is it free?

The library import is free forever. Optional upgrades remove sponsored deal cards and cover deep achievement syncs that hit store APIs on your behalf.

Isn't scraping against the rules?

BAKLOG automates requests you could make yourself, in your own browser, with your own credentials, on your own machine.

Why invite-only right now?

We are onboarding in small waves so we can fix what breaks on real setups before opening the doors. Request an invite and you are in line for the next wave.

Do you sell my data?

No. There is nothing to sell. Your library never leaves your device, and there is no server to breach, sell, or subpoena.
