Questions

Honest answers.

Is it safe?

Your credentials stay encrypted on your machine. Store fetches use your own browser session and your IP. There is no BAKLOG server holding your logins.

Is it free?

Yes. Importing your library across every store is free forever: auto-fetch on connect, full dashboard, ownership-aware deals (with sponsored slots in deal cards on the free tier), and cached trophy summary % (no on-demand deep re-pull). Claimable Now on the free tier surfaces full-game giveaways only — DLC packs, add-ons, and in-game bonuses are filtered out. Store refresh on the free tier is one store at a time, on demand; auto-refresh quietly updates one stale store every ~30 min while the app is open. The optional $5/mo paid tier ($50/yr, planned) will add queued bulk refresh (every stale store in one sweep), scheduled refresh without the app open, no sponsored deal cards, cloud sync, deep achievement/trophy sync (full re-pull), and a bonus claimables feed for those DLC and bonus drops. Nothing paywalled today stays paywalled when paid launches.

What's in the paid tier?

$5/mo or $50/yr (planned): no sponsored deal cards, cloud sync across machines, queued bulk refresh — queue every stale store and let them run back-to-back instead of babysitting one at a time, scheduled stale-store refresh without keeping the app open, deep achievement/trophy sync (full on-demand re-pull; free tier shows cached % only), deal/watchlist alerts, and a bonus claimables feed for DLC, add-ons, and in-game bonuses filtered out of the free feed. The free tier keeps everything you need to import, browse, and decide what to play next.

What's the difference between free and paid refresh?

On the free tier, you refresh one store at a time — click a fetcher chip, wait for it to finish, click the next. Auto-refresh (on by default) quietly updates one stale store every ~30 minutes while the app is open. The planned paid tier adds queued bulk refresh: one action queues every store that needs updating, and they run back-to-back so you can kick it off and walk away. Paid also adds scheduled refresh that runs even when the app is closed (tray or OS scheduler). Your credentials and data still stay on your machine — only the convenience layer changes.

Does BAKLOG show me free games to claim?

Yes. Claimable Now is a maintainer-curated free-game feed that aggregates active giveaways from Epic, GOG, Steam, Prime, and more — sourced via GamerPower, IsThereAnyDeal, and the Epic Store — right in your dashboard. Epic's weekly free game can be claimed from the Epic Games Store Mobile app on supported devices — no computer required — and the mobile store runs its own giveaways too. A notification banner alerts you when new drops land. The feed is ownership-aware — it skips games you already own somewhere in your library. Included on the free tier.

I don't really own games yet - is BAKLOG still for me?

Yes. You don't need a paid library to start. Steam free-to-play, Epic's weekly free game (claimable from the Epic Games Store Mobile app on supported devices — no PC needed), Prime Gaming key drops, GOG's periodic giveaways, StarCraft on Battle.net, Rainbow Six Siege on Ubisoft, and pay-what-you-want itch.io indies are all free entry points. BAKLOG tracks your collection from the first claim — and Claimable Now aggregates the week's free drops from Epic, GamerPower, and IsThereAnyDeal so you never miss one.

Do I have to refresh it?

Not manually. When you connect a store, BAKLOG auto-fetches its library. While the app is open, auto-refresh for stores older than 24h is on by default (Connections). ITAD deal prices refresh on a schedule. There is no background sync while BAKLOG is closed and no cloud sync between machines.

Why is my count different from what the store shows?

Stores pad your library with things that aren't games: DLC skins, soundtracks, wallpapers, betas, and store apps. BAKLOG filters those out automatically so the number you see is real games, not shelf clutter. That filter is built-in and you never have to manage it. Separately, you can hide any game you just don't want to see and restore it later from the Hidden games panel.

Isn't scraping against the rules?

BAKLOG automates requests you could make yourself, in your own browser, with your own credentials, on your own machine.

Why invite-only right now?

We are onboarding in small waves so we can fix what breaks on real setups before opening the doors. Request an invite and you are in line for the next wave.

Do you sell my data?

No. There is nothing to sell. Your library never leaves your device, and there is no server to breach, sell, or subpoena.
