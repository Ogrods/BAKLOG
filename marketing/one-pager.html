Problem

PC gamers accumulate libraries they never intentionally bought — Epic weekly frees, Prime drops, bundle keys, itch megabundles, and dormant accounts they forgot they had. No storefront offers cross-store ownership truth or ownership-aware deal alerts.

Solution

BAKLOG runs on the user's PC (127.0.0.1). It goes to each store on your behalf, opens a direct channel from the storefront to your machine, and normalizes what arrives into one table; ITAD pricing layers deals only when actionable. The 60-second "watch your library appear" demo is onboarding.

Product (shipped)

  • 12 library + 8 wishlist fetchers, cross-store dedupe
  • Ownership-aware deal radar + FX display currency
  • Switchable local profiles (work vs play)
  • Drift guard, atomic writes, honest fetcher health UI
  • Trophy/achievement enrichment (summary free; deep API sync metered)

Business model

Lead: Freemium free + ads in deal surfaces. Optional $2–$4/mo removes ads + adds cloud sync. Affiliate on deal intent. Metered achievement/trophy API enrichment — deep per-title pulls (rarity, unlock timeline) billed by usage via credit packs or bundled in paid tier; free tier keeps cached summary %. ARR floor ~$25k–$50k at 50k MAU (conservative).

Market

Accidental-hoarder persona: near-zero upfront WTP, high deal intent. Epic/Prime/GOG free economies create structural demand for inventory + decision tools.

Competitive edge

  • vs launchers: decision + deals, not launch
  • vs ITAD/GG.deals: ownership context
  • vs Backloggd: auto-import, local-first

Traction / trust
