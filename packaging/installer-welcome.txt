Welcome to BAKLOG

One honest backlog across every store.

This beta build is not code-signed yet. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC" or "Unknown publisher", click More info, then Run anyway.

What installs where
  App folder: %LOCALAPPDATA%\BAKLOG (Start Menu shortcut and tray launcher)
  Your data: %LOCALAPPDATA%\BAKLOG-Data (library, connections, and profiles)

Upgrading or reinstalling keeps your data. Uninstall removes the app; choose whether to keep or delete your library data.

After install, launch BAKLOG from the Start Menu. A tray icon appears in the notification area. Right-click it for Open, Restart, or Quit. You do not need to keep a console window open.

Connections sign-in prefers Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge. If neither is installed, BAKLOG downloads a one-time browser (~150 MB).
