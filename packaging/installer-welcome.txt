Welcome to the BAKLOG beta.

Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" or "Unknown publisher" because this build is not code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.

After install, launch BAKLOG from the Start Menu. A tray icon appears in the notification area. Right-click it for Open, Restart, or Quit. You do not need to keep a console window open.

Connections sign-in requires Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.

Your library data stays on this PC in %LOCALAPPDATA%\BAKLOG-Data (separate from the app folder). Uninstalling BAKLOG removes only the app; your library and connections remain until you delete that data folder.
