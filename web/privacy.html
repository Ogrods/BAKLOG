Privacy

Last updated: 2026-11-06. Full inventory (paths, bug-bundle whitelist, network hosts): PRIVACY.md on GitHub. Keep this page and that file in sync when either changes.

BAKLOG is a local-first desktop tool. It does not ship telemetry or analytics by default. Optional anonymous aggregate metrics are opt-in (Connections: share anonymous stats). Game libraries, personal notes, and store credentials stay on your machine unless you turn on an optional hosted feature.

TL;DR

  • Library JSON and Connections secrets stay on your computer by default.
  • Store fetches go from your PC to each storefront, as you, from your IP.
  • baklog.app marketing pages never receive your catalog.
  • We do not sell your library data.

Hosted surfaces (baklog.app)

These are optional and separate from the local dashboard:

What does not happen

  • No telemetry by default in the desktop app.
  • No silent crash uploads - you copy or send a scrubbed bug bundle yourself.
  • No third-party ad scripts; affiliate tags may appear on links BAKLOG builds when enrolled.
  • No automatic multi-machine sync unless you enable Pro cloud sync.

Removing your data

Local data lives under your BAKLOG data root (on Windows installs, typically under LocalAppData). Delete profile folders, cache, and browser localStorage for the app origin to remove local data. For Pro cloud objects, use Clear cloud library in Connections (turning Cloud sync off stops new uploads but leaves existing cloud files). For hosted account email or other account storage, contact dan@baklog.app.
